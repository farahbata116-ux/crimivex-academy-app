## Applied Branding Rules

1. Official CRIMIVEX Academy logo & title block.
2. Official Tagline: `Criminology • Criminal Justice • Forensic Science • Cybersecurity`.
3. Document Serial / Identification Code (e.g., `CRIM-DOC-2026-XXXX`).
4. Official Contact Details (`farahbata116@gmail.com`, Founder & Instructor Farah Abdi Hassan).
5. Mandatory Legal Disclaimer stating CRIMIVEX Academy is an independent professional education platform issuing certificates of completion.
