# Known Limitations

This document states the limits of what is currently verified and what cannot be claimed.

## Verified facts

- The codebase implements a comprehensive educational platform UI, course content, AI features, and a simulated enrollment flow.
- The live Vercel deployment exists and is connected to the GitHub repository `farahbata116-ux/crimivex-academy-app` on branch `main`.
- The checkout flow is simulated and not connected to a genuine payment provider.
- No study guide PDF was located in the reviewed repo or referenced by a verified asset path.
- No persistence layer was found.
- No real user authentication or authorization layer was found.

## Cannot be claimed without evidence

The following claims are not supported by current evidence:

- Real customer acquisition
- Real revenue or profit
- Real user registrations
- Real product sales
- Real payment transactions
- Real study guide delivery
- Real certificate issuance pipeline tied to backend persistence
- Real access control for paid downloads
- Real CRM or billing integration
- Real business registration or accreditation verification

## Buyer-facing position

The project should be presented as:
- a strong concept/demo with educational content and UX
- a platform that has established a product concept and interface
- a product that still requires payment integration, product delivery, backend persistence, and operational hardening before it can be treated as a real commerce system

## Important note

This is not a claim that the business is incomplete or unviable. It is a statement that the current evidence supports a prototype / product concept rather than a fully operational sale-ready paid commerce asset.
